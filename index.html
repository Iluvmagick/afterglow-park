MOVEMENT TIPS

  • Air strafe: in the air, hold A or D (not W) and turn the mouse the same way smoothly. Chain hops to build speed.
  • Slide hop: sprint, hit SHIFT to slide, jump out of it. Downhill slides speed you up.
  • Wallrun: jump at a wall at an angle while holding W. Jump again to kick off. Wallruns refresh your double jump.
  • Surf: drop onto the pink ramps in the east and hold A/D into the ramp. Do not press W.
  • Rocket jump: jump, look down, fire. Shoot walls to blast yourself sideways.
  • Surfaces: blue ice has no grip, pink trampolines bounce you (hold SPACE to pump, crouch to land), glowing speed strips fling you, green goo lets you crawl wherever you look.
  • The Obelisk (south-east): low gravity, sticky faces, and a ring express that spirals to its halo. Reach the summit.
  • The Mega-Parthenon (far west, past the acropolis and the highway) has two relaxed parkour routes: the Peristyle Climb (from the top of the east ramp up to the pediment's peak) and the Naos Run inside (from the north door up to Athena's helmet).
  • The megacity surrounds the park: each side of the wall has a greek gateway with speed lanes and a launch pad. Ride the Vaporway, take the Sky Elevator up the supertall (north), drop down the Sail (east). Green glass is crawlable.
  • Collect the golden orbs hidden around the park. Orange pads launch you to the zones.
